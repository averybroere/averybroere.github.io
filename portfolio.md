## VirtuaCreature
#### 2021 | Programmer / Game Designer
<iframe src="https://store.steampowered.com/widget/1394240/" frameborder="0" width="646" height="190"></iframe>

A monster simulation & battler inspired by Tamagotchi. I handled the bulk of the programming and the prelimary design/planning, while [Usman Khan](https://usmankhang.github.io/) created all the original pixel art, and a small additional group provided music, testing and insight. The game continued to receive updates until early 2025.

VirtuaCreature was built using Adobe AIR, and utilized an online ecosystem, built with Java/MySQL and SFS2X, for in-game giveaways, events, and multiplayer matches, at its peak hosting over 10,000 accounts across PC, Android & iOS platforms.

## My Building
#### 2026 | Programmer
<iframe src="https://store.steampowered.com/widget/3566300/" frameborder="0" width="646" height="190"></iframe>

My Building is a simple idle game/tech demo made to showcase and expand our internal technology. The game uses Steam's inventory system, where users are granted randomly generated skins for their building, which they can equip and view in-game. Under the hood, the game uses a custom asset encryption library, and our own forks of away3d (for rendering), and the Haxe Steamworks library (for inventory implementation).

## [BytePack](https://github.com/ZeroIPDev/bytepack)
An asset encryption library for the Haxe framework OpenFL. Allows developers to automatically protect production builds using AES-256 ciphers, with a unified single class to load and decrypt the data.
